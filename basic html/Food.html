How to make a 10-Minute delicious chocalate cake?

Ingredients

  • step:1 Four tablespoons flour
  • step:2 Two tablespoons sugar
  • step:3 One tablespoons coco powder
  • step:4 Three tablespoons milk
  • step:5 One tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients together in a large mug.
  2. Add the milk and melted butter; stire until smooth.
  3. Microwave on high powder for 70 to 90 seconds.
  4. Let this cool for 2 minutes before eating.
